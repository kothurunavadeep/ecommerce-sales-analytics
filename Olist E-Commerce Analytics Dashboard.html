Key insights

  • Revenue grew +141% YoY (Jan–Aug 2018 vs 2017); Black Friday 2017 was the peak month.
  • 97% of customers never reorder; month-1 retention is about 0.5%.
  • Orders 8+ days late average 1.7 stars versus 4.3 for early deliveries.
  • SP + RJ + MG make up 63% of sales; the top 10% of sellers earn 67% of revenue.
  • Credit cards carry 78% of value; installment orders are ~3.6× larger (R$301 vs R$83).
Source: Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset (Kaggle), cleaned in SQL. Revenue = item price of delivered orders (freight excluded). On-time = delivered on or before the promised date.
